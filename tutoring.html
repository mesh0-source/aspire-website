What You Receive

What every tutoring client receives

Every tutoring plan includes the following as standard.

  • Learning plan: a clear outline of topics and goals after your first session.
  • Live, interactive sessions: screen sharing, worked examples, and time for your questions.
  • Your own materials: we teach using your course, data, and drafts.
  • Session notes: a short summary of what we covered and what to practice.
  • Practice exercises: tasks between sessions to build confidence.
  • Software walkthroughs: step-by-step guidance in SPSS, R, Stata, or Excel.
  • Email support: quick questions answered between sessions (bundles and coaching).
  • Flexible scheduling: times that work for US, European, and African time zones.

FAQ

Common questions

What times are sessions available?

We're based in Nairobi (EAT, UTC+3), so we can offer morning and early-afternoon sessions for US clients and daytime sessions for clients in Africa and Europe.

Can you use my course materials?

Yes, and we encourage it. Bring your syllabus, readings, datasets, or drafts and we'll work through them together.

Can sessions be recorded?

Yes, on request, so you can review them later.

Do you offer a trial?

We offer a free 15-minute introductory call to make sure we're the right fit before you book.

How do I pay for sessions?

Sessions are paid in advance by PayPal or another agreed method. Bundles and coaching are paid at the start of each package or month.

Can I reschedule a session?

Yes. Sessions can be rescheduled free of charge with at least 24 hours' notice.

Ready to start learning?

Tell us the subject, your level, and when you're available. We'll suggest a time for a free introductory call.
