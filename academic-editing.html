What You Receive

What you receive with every edit

Every editing project includes the following as standard.

  • Track Changes version: every edit visible, so you approve each change.
  • Clean version: a copy with all changes accepted, ready to submit.
  • Margin comments: explanations and questions where your input is needed.
  • Consistency check: spelling (US or UK), terminology, abbreviations, and number style.
  • Style guide compliance: APA, Harvard, MLA, Chicago, Vancouver, or your journal's rules.
  • Editor's summary: a short note on recurring issues to help your future writing.
  • Free re-check: a quick look at sections you revise after the edit.
  • Confidentiality: your unpublished work is never shared.

FAQ

Common questions

Will I see what you changed?

Yes. You receive a Track Changes version showing every edit and a clean version with all changes accepted.

Is editing allowed for my thesis?

Most universities allow proofreading and language editing, and some limit substantive editing. Please check your institution's policy. We'll match the level of editing to what's permitted.

Do you check references?

Yes. In the Professional and Journal-Ready packages we check that references are complete, consistently formatted, and match the in-text citations.

How fast can you edit?

Typical turnaround is 3–5 business days for up to 10,000 words. Faster delivery is available for an additional fee.

Do you edit in US or UK English?

Either. Tell us which one your university or journal requires and we'll apply it consistently.

Can I send one chapter first?

Yes. Many clients start with one chapter to see our editing before sending the full thesis or manuscript.

Ready to polish your manuscript?

Send your document's word count, the level of editing you need, and your deadline. We'll send an exact quote.
