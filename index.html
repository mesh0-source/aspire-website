What We Do

Three areas of support, one integrated practice

Aspire provides research-informed, funder-aligned support across three core service lines — and combines them for organizations that need more comprehensive support.

Grant Development

Funder-aligned proposals

Helping organizations identify funding opportunities and develop funder-aligned proposals — from concept notes through full narratives, budget justifications, and final review.

Research

Evidence-based analysis

Producing evidence-based research, analysis, assessments, and reports — desk research, literature reviews, needs assessments, and research synthesis.

Technical Documentation

Professional documentation

Developing professional reports, briefs, program documents, and organizational materials — technical reports, policy briefs, white papers, and impact reports.
