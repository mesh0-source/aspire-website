Our standards

FAQ

Common questions

Will I get the code or syntax?

Yes. Every project includes the SPSS syntax, R script, Stata do-file, or MATLAB/Python code, so the analysis is fully reproducible.

Can you help me choose the right test?

Yes. If you're unsure, we start with a written analysis plan that matches each research question to an appropriate method and explains why.

Is my data kept confidential?

Yes. Your data is used only for your project and deleted on request when it's complete. We sign an NDA if you need one.

What if my supervisor or a reviewer asks questions about the analysis?

Professional and Strategic packages include follow-up support to answer questions and make reasonable revisions.

Have data you need analyzed?

Send your research questions, a description of your dataset, and your deadline. We'll reply within two business days with a plan and quote.
