What we ghostwrite, and what we don't

Our ghostwriting is for professional, business, and published work. We don't write coursework, theses, dissertations, or exams that will be submitted for academic credit. For academic work, see our Academic Editing and Tutoring services.

FAQ

Common questions

Will anyone know you wrote it?

No. We never claim credit, list the work in our portfolio, or mention you as a client without your written permission. An NDA is available on request.

Who owns the copyright?

You do. All rights transfer to you once the final payment is made.

How do you make it sound like me?

We start with an interview, study anything you've already written or recorded, and refine the draft with you until the voice is right.

Can you write for my organization rather than for me personally?

Yes. We write blogs, reports, and newsletters in an organization's voice as well as for individuals.

Have something you want to say?

Tell us what you want to write and where it will be published. We'll reply within two business days.
