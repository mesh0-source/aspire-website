What You Receive

Everything you need to submit with confidence

Every grant writing project includes the following as standard.

  • Guidelines analysis: a breakdown of the funder's requirements, eligibility rules, and scoring criteria.
  • Compliance checklist: every required section, attachment, and word or page limit, checked off.
  • Evidence-backed need statement: current data and research that show why your program matters.
  • SMART objectives: specific, measurable outcomes the funder can track.
  • Editable files: Word documents ready to paste into online portals, with a clean PDF copy.
  • Submission notes: a short list of anything you still need to attach, sign, or upload.
  • Two revision rounds: your feedback included before the final version.
  • Reusable content: sections you can adapt for future applications.

FAQ

Common questions

Do you guarantee that we will be funded?

No. Funding decisions belong to the funder, and no honest grant writer can guarantee an award. We guarantee a proposal that is complete, compliant with the guidelines, and as competitive as your program allows.

Do you work for a percentage of the grant?

No. We charge flat, agreed fees. Commission-based grant writing is considered unethical by professional grant-writing associations, and most funders do not allow grant funds to pay for it.

Can you meet a tight deadline?

Often, yes. Rush delivery is available for an additional 25–50%, depending on the timeline. Contact us as early as possible with the deadline and guidelines.

What do you need from us?

Your mission, program details, budget, any past reports or proposals, and the funder's guidelines. Our intake form collects everything in one place.

Do you work with organizations outside the US?

Yes. We work with US-registered nonprofits and with NGOs internationally, including organizations running programs in Kenya and East Africa.

Can you submit the application for us?

Applications must be submitted by your organization through your own funder account. We prepare everything to be ready to upload and can guide you through the portal on a call.

Can you write federal grants?

Yes. We prepare narratives for federal and state applications. These are larger projects with strict formats, so they are quoted under the Strategic package.

Have a funding deadline coming up?

Send us the funder's guidelines and your deadline. We'll reply within two business days with a scope and quote.
