What You Receive

A finished report, not just a draft

Every report project includes the following as standard.

  • Agreed outline: structure and headings approved by you before writing begins.
  • Executive summary: a one-to-two page summary for busy readers and boards.
  • Credible sources: peer-reviewed research, official statistics, and reputable reports, fully referenced.
  • Tables & figures: clear charts and tables that make your data easy to read.
  • Actionable recommendations: practical next steps linked directly to the findings.
  • Professional formatting: consistent headings, numbering, and layout, ready to share.
  • Editable files: Word and PDF versions, plus chart source files on request.
  • Revision rounds: one or two rounds depending on the package.

FAQ

Common questions

Can you work with our raw data?

Yes. We can clean and analyze your data and present the results in the report. See our Statistical & Data Analysis service for the methods and software we use.

What formats do you deliver?

Editable Microsoft Word files and print-ready PDFs as standard. Google Docs and presentation slides are available on request.

Is our information kept confidential?

Yes. We sign a confidentiality agreement on request, and we never share or publish client material without written permission.

Which citation styles do you use?

APA by default, or any style your organization or funder requires.

Can you follow our organization's template or branding?

Yes. Send us your template, logo, and brand colors and we'll format the report to match.

Can you turn the report into slides or a summary?

Yes. Presentation slides, one-page summaries, and infographics can be added to any report.

Need a report written up?

Tell us what the report is for, who will read it, and your deadline. We'll send a scope and quote within two business days.
